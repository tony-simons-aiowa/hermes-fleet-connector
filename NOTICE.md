# Provenance

MIT licensed. The pairing store `fleet_connector/devices.py` and its original store tests derive from the locally authored Hermes Fleet pairing candidate `1bffff90b482dba75aeb9c55a5fb704c74c75841`, based on NousResearch/hermes-agent. The private renderer derives from the Hermes Fleet host-tools source at `f021b0b3a0822f675305eca9919a0c90f99905af`, with shortened display timeout and standalone imports. This is a materially separate external connector/service, not a fork or listing of a bundled plugin. Source attribution and license are retained. No Nous endorsement is claimed.

The 0.1.2 browser viewer replaces the Quick Look presentation layer. Its Fleet wing asset is the existing Hermes Fleet `FleetWingMark` PNG from source `f021b0b3a0822f675305eca9919a0c90f99905af`, reused for this local candidate; no new or substituted logo was generated. Public distribution remains gated on the owner's test and approval.
